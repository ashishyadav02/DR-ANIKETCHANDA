ABOUT

Prof. Dr. Aniket Chanda

Dr. Aniket Chanda is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Civil Engineering at Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology (MANIT), Bhopal. His research focuses on Computational Solid Mechanics, Structural Mechanics, Higher-Order Plate and Shell Theories, Finite Element Methods, Composite Structures, Functionally Graded Materials, and Differential Quadrature Methods. His work emphasizes developing efficient numerical models for advanced engineering structures.
